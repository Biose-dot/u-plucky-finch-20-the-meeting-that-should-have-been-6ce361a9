— also listed at https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9.

One line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny.
The house judge reads entries two at a time and keeps the funnier line.

> The meeting that should have been an email ran an hour and ended with one decision: send an email about the meeting.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) copies `entry.txt` to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
